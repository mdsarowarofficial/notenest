NoteNest — Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

NoteNest is a notebook app for students. This policy explains what it collects, where that information goes, who can see it, and how to delete it. It describes the app as it is actually built; nothing here is boilerplate.

NoteNest is run by Md Sarowar Hossain. Questions, requests and complaints: Seyamhossainpop@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Your notes, subjects, exams, photos, PDFs and recordings are stored on your phone. They leave it only when you choose to share a note.
  • The cloud account is optional. You need it only to share notes with classmates, join study groups and classes, and chat.
  • Google Drive is used only if you turn on backup or post a file to a class, and then only for files NoteNest itself saves there.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers in the app, and your information is never sold.
  • You can delete your account, and everything on your phone, from inside the app: Profile → Delete account.

2. What stays on your phone

When you create an account in NoteNest, these are kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your name, email address and (if you give it) your institution;
  • your password, stored only as a salted, slow hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256), never as the password itself;
  • your notes, subjects, exams, reminders, settings and cover-page drafts;
  • photos, PDFs and voice recordings you attach to notes.

This data is not encrypted by the app, so anyone who can unlock your phone can read it. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in that backup; that backup is controlled by your phone's settings and by Google, not by NoteNest.

The operator can read cloud data. Through the Firebase console, the person running NoteNest can technically see everything stored in the cloud, including shared notes and messages. We look at it only to investigate reports, fix problems, or when the law requires it. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored encrypted by Google.

Google Drive

If you turn on Google Drive backup, or attach a file to a class post or to work you hand in, NoteNest asks Google for permission to use your Drive with the drive.file scope. That permission lets the app see and change only the files NoteNest itself creates — never the rest of your Drive. Files go in a folder called NoteNest in your own Drive, where you can open, move or delete them like any other file.

  • Backup: one file holding your notes, subjects, exams, settings and attachments. It is private to your Drive and is not shared with anyone.
  • Class files: a file you post to a class, or hand in, is set to "anyone with the link can view", and the link is stored in that class in the cloud, where only its members (or, for handed-in work, only the teacher and you) can read it. Anyone you pass the link to yourself could open the file. Deleting a post or a class does not delete the file from your Drive; you can delete it there.

NoteNest has no server of its own: the app talks to Google Drive directly from your phone, and the operator never receives your Drive files or your Drive access.

4. Microphone and dictation

NoteNest asks for the microphone only when you record a voice note or use dictation. Recordings are saved on your phone. Dictation uses your phone's own speech-recognition service. On most Android phones that is Google's, which may send the audio to Google to turn it into text, unless an offline language pack is installed. That is how Android works; NoteNest does not receive or keep that audio.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No access to your location, contacts, call logs or other apps' files.

6. Safety: reporting and blocking

Hold any message, or open the menu on a conversation or shared note, to report it or block its sender. You must not send anything abusive, hateful, sexual or illegal. We review reports and may remove content or delete accounts that break these rules.

7. Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Delete account. You will be asked to confirm with your password, or with Google if you sign in with Google. This deletes:

  • everything NoteNest stored on your phone;
  • your cloud profile, your email lookup entry and your Firebase sign-in;
  • study groups and classes you created (for all their members), with a class's posts and handed-in work; you are removed from other groups and classes;
  • notes you have sent that are still waiting, and notes waiting for you;
  • your block list.

It does not delete messages you already sent in conversations, or notes other people already accepted into their own notebooks. Those now belong to the other people's copies, under the name you used when you sent them. Work you handed in to someone else's class stays with that class, for its teacher, until the teacher deletes the assignment or the class. Reports you made are kept for safety review. Files in your Google Drive are yours and stay there until you delete them in Drive.

If you cannot use the app, email Seyamhossainpop@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete the cloud account for you.

8. Children

NoteNest is meant for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
